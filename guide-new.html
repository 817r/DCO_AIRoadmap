What do you need?

Load the mod and every AI already fights better — nothing to set up. Everything below is a question people actually ask, with the answer in place.

Just installed it

Five minutes in, nothing exploded, now what.

I loaded the mod but nothing seems different

It is working — DCO changes how AI thinks, not how it looks. Put yourself in a firefight and watch for these:

  • Soldiers break for cover instead of standing and trading shots
  • They dive when rounds come close, and stay down a moment
  • One squad shouts a contact and a nearby squad reacts to it
  • A squad that is losing falls back rather than dying in place

If none of that happens, the mod is not actually loading. Check it is enabled for the scenario, not just subscribed.

Next: the thing most people change first is how hard the AI is — the two questions below.
The AI is too hard — how do I make it easier?

Press F2 in Game Master and load the Forgiving preset. That is the one-click answer.

By hand, the setting that matters most is aiAggression — lower it to about 0.7. One warning that catches everyone: raising aggression quietly scales down take-cover, dodge and suppression, so set aggression first and judge everything else after.

On a server: the same preset exists as a config file you can drop in.
Server templates The aggression trap
The AI is too easy — how do I make it harder?

Press F2 and load Realistic, or raise these three:

  • aiSkillDefault to Veteran
  • aimAccuracy to about 1.3
  • Personality weights toward Aggressive and Reckless

Leave suppressionEffect alone at first. Lowering it makes AI nearly impossible to pin, which reads as unfair rather than hard.

Playing as a soldier

You are on the ground and there is an AI commander in the mission.

How do I give orders to the AI commander?

Through the same group command menu you already use for your own squad. Aim at a position or an enemy, open the menu, pick a DCO command. Everything goes to your faction's commander, which decides what to do with it.

You can report contacts (team, squad, platoon, vehicle, armor), ask for suppression, support, a flank, armor, transport, extraction or MEDEVAC, and volunteer your squad for a job — assault, fix, block or recon.

The commander remembers you. Finish the tasks you accept and your requests get favoured. Abandon them repeatedly and you go on cooldown.
How do I call in artillery?

Mark the target, pick the shell type and how many rounds. The commander has to approve it and may give you fewer than you asked for.

  • HE — 1, 3, 5 or 8 rounds. Needs an observed target.
  • Smoke — 1, 3 or 5. Falls back to a rough area mission if nobody has eyes on.
  • Illumination — 1, 2 or 3. Worth it before a night assault.

After an approved mission your group is on cooldown: roughly 30s + 10s per round for smoke and illumination, 60s + 20s per round for HE.

My fire mission was refused or cut short

The radio call names the reason. There are six:

  • No observation — HE is refused outright; smoke and illumination drop to an imprecise area mission
  • Friendlies near the target — HE refused
  • Not enough tubes — cut to the guns available
  • Queue busy — cut to about half
  • Cautious commander — HE capped at 5
  • Danger close — HE capped at 3 when you are near the impact area
Report from closer. A contact report carries position error that grows with distance — roughly 12 m for every 100 m. That error decides whether artillery can be aimed precisely.
The commander ignores my requests

Three likely reasons, in order:

  1. Your group is still on fire-mission cooldown from the last one
  2. Your reliability is low after abandoning tasks the commander gave you
  3. There is no commander for your faction in the mission at all

Running it in Game Master

Editing a live session. Changes last until the session ends.

How do I set up two AI sides fighting over objectives?

Five minutes, no Workbench needed.

  1. Place an AI Commander for each side. Set its Faction and give it a short Name — both are required.
  2. Place two or more AI Commander Objectives. Name, radius (a town 100–150 m, a farm 40–60 m) and Controlled By for whoever starts with it.
  3. Place groups near their side and set each group's Commander.
  4. Press F2 and load a preset — Recommended to start.
  5. Unpause. First orders arrive after about a minute.
Next: give one side a town to hold, or reinforcements — both are a few questions down.
Do it in the Workbench instead
My commander does nothing

Work down this list — it is almost always one of the first two.

  1. Faction is empty. It then rejects every group. The start-up check logs faction=EMPTY.
  2. Groups never joined it. In Game Master each group needs its Commander attribute set; in the Workbench it needs Auto Assign ticked.
  3. It has no objective it is allowed to take. Check Handled By on the objectives.
  4. You did not wait. See the next question.
Read the start-up check first. About 20 seconds in, DCO checks the scene and tells every Game Master if something is wrong. It catches the missing faction, the missing name, an objective nobody handles and a missing navmesh — most of this list.
Nothing happens for the first minute

That is normal, not a bug. The commander waits out a first-think delay of 60 s, then thinks on an interval.

Worth knowing: the shipped Workbench commander prefab uses a 150 s think interval, not the 30 s script default. On a small test mission that feels like it died. Lower it.

How do I make a squad hold a town?

Switch to command mode, select the group, and place the Garrison (DCO) waypoint on the building or position. Its settings dialog opens by itself and waits up to 60 s while you set it up.

Pick the mode: Hold never leaves the building, Defend allows a small flank team out, Release garrisons until a trigger frees the group.

It degrades gracefully. No building means field fighting positions; failing that it behaves like a vanilla Defend waypoint.

How do I make a squad clear a building?

Command mode, select the group, place the Do CQB (DCO) waypoint within 25 m of the building. The group clears it room by room. You never place an indoor navigation graph — DCO builds one from the soldier navmesh at runtime.

If it ends immediately: no building within 25 m, the building has no outside door or no rooms on the navmesh, or the group has fewer than two men.
How do I give a side reinforcements?

Place an AI Commander Spawner behind that side's line and pick a preset — the preset fills in the trigger for you.

Match the preset to the side. Counterattack wave fires when an objective your side holds is lost, so it only works for a defender. An attacker that owns nothing can never trigger it — give attackers Commander on call or Steady top-up.
My spawner never spawns
  • A player or enemy is inside its 300 m safe radius — this is what stops men appearing in front of players
  • The faction AI cap is reached
  • The commander's stock is empty
  • It is still on cooldown

Right-click › Spawn anyway skips the safe radius once, but never the AI cap.

Next: if men spawn but never join the fight, that is a commander problem — search does nothing.
An objective never changes hands

Control only advances while some commander is actively planning that objective. One nobody handles will sit there forever no matter how many soldiers stand in it.

Check Handled By on the objective, and that Allowed Factions does not exclude the attacker.

The AI does not move at all

No navmesh, or the objective sits off it. AI cannot walk where there is no soldier navmesh, and indoor plans cannot be built either.

The start-up check reports it as navmesh=missing_world for the world, or navmesh=none per objective.

Can I see what the commander is thinking?

Yes — Ctrl + I opens the intent overlay, on the map or in the world view. Enable it first under Global › Performance & Debug; it is Game Master only and never shown to players.

It draws objectives with owner and confidence, the commander's current focus and why, believed enemy positions, group lines coloured by role, and a side card with its last six decisions.

Hovering works too: a group shows its task, posture, threat and morale; a soldier shows personality, skill and leadership.

Running a server

Tuning DCO without opening an editor.

Where are the settings on a server?

A folder of JSON files at $profile:DCO/Config/, written on the first start with every setting at its current value. Edit, restart, done.

Core, Soldiers, Squads, Commander and Vehicles each get a file, plus Advanced/ for the tuning keys and Factions/ for per-faction values.

Every key, with templates
I changed a config value and nothing happened
  • A typo. A misspelled key is not an error — it is simply unknown, and it disappears at the next rewrite
  • You edited while the server ran. The config is read at startup, and a later write overwrites your file
  • It was clamped to its valid range, or migrated because it still sat at an old default

Grep dco.log for cfg_clamped, cfg_bad_type, cfg_parse_fail and cfg_unused.

My Game Master changes vanish after a restart

Expected — Game Master edits apply live but are not written back. To keep them, use Save to server config if your server allows it (gmSaveConfig is admin by default), or save a settings preset.
