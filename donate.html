// FAQ

Questions

Is DCO_AI free?

Yes. DCO_AI is free on the Arma Reforger Workshop, and donations are optional.

What are donations used for?

Development time and the hardware to build and test on. Funding tiers map to blocks of the roadmap, so you can see what your support moves forward.

How do I pay?

Through Ko-fi, which handles the payment. We never see your card details.

When does my name show up?

Within 24 hours of your donation. If you'd rather not be listed, or want a different callsign, leave a note with your donation or message us on Discord.

Can I help without donating?

Absolutely. Play it, report bugs and share videos on Discord — or apply for an open position.
